Detailed exterior edition
Building materials and visible facade rhythms for all 35 campus codes were interpreted from
KMUTT Building and Ground Management exterior photos. The KMUTT Bangmod Virtual
Tour was consulted for supplementary exterior angles. Dimensions, elevations without
photographs, and roofs are modeled estimates. Images remain at source.
https://bgm.kmutt.ac.th/MapNavi/MAPNAVI.html
https://www.kmutt.ac.th/virtual/bangmod/

LX podium and roof references include the KMUTT SoA+D publication
On Physical University, the rooftop solar study and campus photographs.
The model uses independently drawn geometry and approximate dimensions.
https://soad.kmutt.ac.th/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/60_25_On-Physical-University_eboook.pdf#page=66
https://directivepublications.org/journal-of-environmental-and-sciences/analysis-of-solar-power-generation-system-for-green-building-prototype-in-king-mongkuts-university-of-technology-thonburi
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:KMUTT_View.jpg
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:KMUTT_Bangmod_4.jpg

Building sign lettering was drawn from transcriptions of exterior photographs
using Outfit and Noto Sans Thai under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Source references for the signs are stored on the building nodes of the GLB.

The accompanying site-context.geojson contains campus drive and path centerlines
from OpenStreetMap (ODbL 1.0). Keep it available with the detailed GLB. Widths,
paint markings, facade dimensions and most landscape placements are schematic.
walking-network.json contains a filtered OpenStreetMap snapshot (ODbL 1.0)
with way and node IDs for the walking graph, plus reviewed gate and approach
references. Gate locations and access tags are map observations, without a field survey.
Google Maps satellite view is linked for independent comparison on Google Maps.
The GLB, render and geographic dataset use the independently attributed sources above.
